# Design document rules

- The Decisions log in `gdd.md` is append-only: date, decision, why, rejected alternatives.
- Milestones and acceptance criteria are measurable (numbers, scenario names, screenshot names), not adjectives.
- Task packets list owned paths, not-allowed paths and the verification tier.
- Feel targets are numbers (seconds, frames, pixels); "snappy" is not a target.
